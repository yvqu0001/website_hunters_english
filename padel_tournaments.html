Padel Tournaments

Basic UX/UI

Under the theme “Basic UX/UI,” we focused on creating effective user interfaces (UI) and user experiences (UX) throughout the development process, based on various user tests and research methods. The theme was self-selected, and the assignment involved developing a website with the opportunity to test it on the target audience.

A strong emphasis was placed on becoming more familiar with Figma, a widely used tool in the field of multimedia design. We worked with fundamental elements in Figma, such as creating components, and these were used to develop a clickable prototype that could later be used for user testing prior to implementation.

Research

Following research interviews with both players and organizers, this solution was identified: to develop a platform that could be used both to find and create padel tournaments.

There was a need for a website where users could create all three types of tournaments while also being able to find and browse them.

Styletile

The website is designed with padel as its point of departure—blue tones are used to reference the court, while neon yellow represents the ball. In addition, darker colors are applied to create a cool, sporty expression.

The visual style is kept simple and minimalist to avoid unnecessary visual noise, and transparency is used to achieve a modern and clean look.
